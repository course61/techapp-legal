TechApp Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

In short. TechApp is a work app for HVAC and appliance technicians. Your jobs, photos and money records are stored on a private server run by the developer, not on a big advertising platform. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no sale of data. You can delete your account and everything on the server from inside the app.

1. Who is responsible

TechApp is made and operated by an individual developer in the United States ("I", "me"). Contact: techapp.supportca@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores

DataWhy

For email sign-in: your email address and the one-time code (stored only as a hash, valid for 10 minutes). Text on data plates and receipts is read on your phone with Apple's Vision framework; the pictures are not sent anywhere for reading.

3. How the data is used

  • To run the app: sign-in, saving, syncing between your devices and backups.
  • To keep the service secure (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).
  • I do not sell your data, share it for advertising, profile you, or use it to train any AI model.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Your records and photos are stored on a server in the United States that I rent and administer. The app connects only to that server, over TLS 1.3, and verifies the server with a private certificate built into the app. The server accepts only connections from the app.
  • Sign-in tokens and one-time codes are stored hashed. The app keeps your sign-in in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted: as the administrator I can technically access the server's contents. I do that only to run, repair and secure the service.
  • No system is perfectly secure, and I cannot promise absolute security.

5. Other companies involved

  • Apple and Google when you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. They tell the app who you are; their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.
  • Google (Gmail) is used to send sign-in code emails. Your email address and the code pass through Google's mail servers.
  • The server hosting provider supplies the machine the server runs on.
  • AI assistant (optional, off by default). If you add your own API key from Anthropic or OpenAI, the question you type is sent directly from your phone to that company under your key and their terms. Nothing else from the app is sent, and the key stays on your device (it is not synced to the server).
  • Parts search. When you tap a parts-search shortcut, your browser opens a parts website with the model number in the address. That is a separate website with its own policy.
  • Maps and phone. When you tap an address, it is handed to the maps app you choose, Apple Maps or Google Maps, to show directions (if the Google Maps app is not installed, Google Maps opens in your browser). When you tap a phone number, it is handed to the Phone app. These apps and websites work under their own policies; the TechApp server does not receive anything from this.
  • Address suggestions. While you type an address for a new job, the text you have typed is sent to Apple's map search (Apple Maps) to offer matching addresses. This only happens while you type in that field, and typing works without it. Apple's own privacy policy applies; the TechApp server does not receive these searches.
  • Reminders and the home-screen widget. Follow-up reminders are scheduled as notifications on your own iPhone; they are not sent through any server or company. The optional widget shows your next scheduled jobs (time, customer name, address, equipment) from a small file kept on your iPhone, shared only between TechApp and its widget. Signing out clears it.

6. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting a job, expense or part removes its content (the app keeps deleted items 30 days in "Recently deleted", then removes them).
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, all records and all photos on the server. Data already on your phone stays there until you delete the app.
  • Photos that no longer belong to any record are removed from the server after 45 days.
  • Safety backups: the server is backed up nightly (copies kept 14 days), and a copy of the server's data is kept on the developer's computer (database copies up to 30 days; photos removed from the server are kept there for up to 45 days). After that, deleted data is gone from backups too.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: everything you entered is shown in the app, and Profile → Backup & restore saves your records to a file (photos are kept on the server and are not inside that file).
  • Correction and deletion: edit or delete anything in the app, or delete the whole account as described above.
  • You can ask me any privacy question or request at techapp.supportca@gmail.com. If you live in a place with specific privacy laws (such as California), you have the rights those laws give you, and I will honor them. I do not sell or share personal information.

8. Children

TechApp is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, I will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app when the change affects how your data is used.
